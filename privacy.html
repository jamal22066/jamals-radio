Privacy

Jamal's Radio · updated 7 November 2026

When you play a station, we log the station, how long you listened and your IP address, so we can see roughly where listeners are (looked up offline with DB-IP). Logs are deleted after 90 days.

No cookies, no ads, no third-party trackers, and nothing is sold or shared. Your favorites and volume stay in your browser. The audio comes straight from each station's own server.
