Privacy

Jamal's Radio · last updated 7 November 2026

Jamal's Radio plays public radio streams from around the world. It has no accounts, no ads, no cookies and no third-party trackers.

What is logged when you play a station

  • Which station you played, when, and for how long, and whether the stream failed to start.
  • Your IP address and the country Cloudflare reports for it.
  • A random ID for that page load, which is not stored on your device and is gone when you close the tab.

The IP address is used to see roughly where listeners are (city, region and network provider, looked up offline in the free DB-IP database) and to stop abuse. This information is used only to understand which stations people listen to. It is not sold, shared or used for advertising.

How long it is kept

Play logs are deleted automatically after 90 days.

Other services involved

  • Cloudflare delivers this site and sees normal web traffic, as for any site it serves.
  • Radio stations: the audio comes straight from each station's own server, which sees your IP address like any website you visit.
  • Radio Browser provides the station list. When you play a station, your browser tells it so it can count the click.
  • NASA GIBS provides the globe imagery.

Stored on your device

Your favorites, volume and a cached station list are kept in your browser's local storage. They never leave your device.
